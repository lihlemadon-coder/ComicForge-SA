## Authentic SA Flavors (use respectfully, research before finalizing)
- Locations: rural KZN, valleys, cattle posts, rivers, homesteads
- Culture: amasi, pap, ancestral traditions, isangoma, tikoloshe, cattle, sticks/knobkerries
- Language: isiZulu primarily; deep Zulu for spiritual scenes, conversational Zulu for daily life
- **Rule:** No stereotypes. Characters are people first. Magic grows from real belief systems.
